# Design

Operate-mode product UI. Light theme chosen for the scene: citizens outdoors on phones in daylight, officers at desks.

- **Type:** IBM Plex Sans with IBM Plex Sans Devanagari (one family covering English and Hindi), fixed rem scale, tabular numerals for all measurements (`num` utility).
- **Surfaces:** warm paper `#f5f4ef`, white work surfaces, a second neutral `#ebe9e1` for municipal navigation.
- **Accent:** deep teal `#0b5d57`, used only for primary actions, current selection and links.
- **Status:** flagged ("Unexpected PM10 excess") = red with a warning icon; within range = green with a check; no assessment = neutral with a hatch texture. Always icon plus label, never colour alone. "No assessment" must never look like "normal".
- **Source tags:** every reading carries its source. Simulated = amber with a flask icon; authenticated device = teal with a tower icon. A strip under the header states that the system is a demonstration.
- **Charts:** one y-axis each. Background = blue `#2a78d6`, site = orange `#eb6834`, difference = violet, barrier = dashed ink. Locality colours are fixed per locality.
- **Signature element:** the barrier scale in `client/src/components/Reading.jsx` (observed difference as a bar, barrier as a marker, calibrated margin as a band).
- **Public map:** the citizen home is a full-bleed map on a pale basemap, with ward chips and search floating over it, a Map/List switch, and one primary action in the bottom bar on phones (Report construction dust). Details open as a bottom sheet on phones and a side panel on wide screens, each with its own URL.
- **Map vocabulary:** shapes that never swap meaning, defined once in `client/src/lib/mapMarkers.js` and used on the citizen and officer maps alike. A dark square is a construction project. The badge on its corner is the DEMO sensor (diamond = PM10 flag, disc = within range, hatched disc = no assessment). A pin is a citizen report, the largest and warmest mark on the map, in a vermilion (`--color-report`) that is neither a status colour nor the accent: filled = open, hollow = closed by the AirWatch desk, with a dark corner mark when residents say the dust persists. Reports at a project stand in an arc above its square. Ward boundaries are thin grey lines with a pale casing, solid for pilot wards and dotted for the rest; teal is kept for controls. Every marker has a white edge and a dark ring so it reads on light and dark tiles, a 44 px hit area, a name, and opens with Enter or Space. The legend is built from the real markers and doubles as the layer switches.
- **Three positions:** a case shows the AirWatch review, the official BMC complaint and what residents see as three rows that are never merged into one status. Entries in the evidence trail carry a tag for whose they are (grey = AirWatch review, teal = official complaint, vermilion = resident) and a line for whether a moderator has checked them.
- **Record and demo tags:** facts from public records carry a teal "Public record" tag with source links and the date checked. Everything simulated or seeded carries the amber flask tag. A demo sensor block sits on an amber wash so it cannot be read as part of the project record.
- **Case and report pages:** two columns from 1024 px. The reporter's case page leads with the three positions side by side and one "what to do next" line with a button that goes there; evidence and the on-site check sit beside the BMC steps, which are three numbered steps that open one at a time. The report form is three numbered sections that tick off, with a summary column (a small map with the same markers and ward lines, the ward office, a checklist). On phones both are one column and secondary cards fold to their heading (`FoldPanel` in `client/src/components/ui.jsx`).
- **Demo images:** photographs on demo cases are synthetic scenes with "DEMO IMAGE" written into the picture, and an amber Demo tag with a one-line note wherever they are shown.
- Tokens live in `client/src/index.css` under `@theme`.
